# Bank Data Analysis – ETL & Market Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end ETL and exploratory data analysis workflow on data related to the world's largest banks.

The project focuses on extracting bank data, transforming and cleaning the dataset, performing exploratory and market-cap analysis, and generating business insights to understand the relative size and market position of major banks.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Extract bank information from a web-based source
- Clean and transform the data for analysis
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Analyse bank rankings and market capitalization
- Identify market concentration and differences in bank size
- Prepare data for dashboard visualization
- Generate actionable business insights

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. **Data Extraction**
   - Retrieved bank data from the archived Wikipedia source containing the list of largest banks.

2. **Data Transformation**
   - Loaded and structured the extracted tables.
   - Cleaned and transformed the data into an analysis-ready format.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis**
   - Analysed bank rankings and market capitalization.
   - Examined the relationship between bank rank and market capitalization.
   - Visualized the distribution and relative size of the banks.

4. **Advanced Analysis**
   - Market capitalization comparison
   - Market concentration analysis
   - Quartile analysis
   - Comparative bank size analysis
   - Growth and gap analysis
   - Dominance scoring

5. **Dashboard Preparation**
   - Prepared a cleaned dataset for dashboard visualization and business reporting.

## 📊 Key Insights

The analysis highlights significant differences in the market capitalization of the largest banks.

The results show that a small number of major banks have substantially larger market capitalization than the remaining banks, indicating concentration among leading institutions.

The analysis also demonstrates the usefulness of combining bank rankings with market capitalization to understand relative market position.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Track changes in market capitalization alongside bank rankings.
- Use cross-currency analysis when comparing banks across regions.
- Monitor market-share concentration among leading institutions.
- Identify regional and segment-level expansion opportunities.
- Use dashboard-based monitoring for ongoing comparison of major banks.
